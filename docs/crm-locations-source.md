# CRM administrative locations

Bundled codes: 34 provinces and 3,321 commune-level units, two-tier 2025 administrative list. Source: https://github.com/ngankt2/vn-location (MIT; retrieved 2026-11-06). Legal reference: Decision 19/2025/QĐ-TTg, effective 2025-07-01; Government publication https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/bang-danh-muc-va-ma-so-cua-34-tinh-thanh-moi-cac-don-vi-hanh-chinh-cap-xa-moi-11925070418263625.htm.

The secondary source's nonstandard “Thủ Đô Hà Nội” and “Thành phố Đồng Nai” labels were normalized to “Thành phố Hà Nội” and “Tỉnh Đồng Nai”; codes retained. No old district is required. Subsequent legal changes are not automatically synchronized; revalidate before replacing this baseline. Province/ward counts and parent links are tested in the project.
